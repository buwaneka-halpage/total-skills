# Encode the lesson

A lesson that lives only in someone's head will be repeated. Put it where it will be enforced, preferring the strongest option that fits:

1. **Code or tooling:** a type, lint rule, test, CI check or pre-commit hook that makes the mistake impossible.
2. **Team context:** a rule in `context/TEAM.md`.
3. **Skill:** a step or check in the relevant `SKILL.md`, or a new skill if the workflow keeps recurring.

## Steps

1. State the lesson in one sentence: "When X, do Y, because Z."
2. Propose where it goes (from the list above) and the exact change.
3. Make the change after the user agrees. Keep skills short: add a line, and delete one that no longer earns its place.
